How to play

Tap bubbles, or swipe through them, to pop them before they break the surface. Every unbroken pop multiplies the next, and they're worth more the higher you let them climb.

Let a bubble escape and it costs a breath. Hit ink and it costs one too, or five seconds against the clock; it also stops your swipe and clouds the water. A pearl gives you a held breath, which saves a chain that would have broken.

The deeper you dive, the stranger the water: the current starts to wander and the light gives out. The higher your score, the faster ink rushes up.
